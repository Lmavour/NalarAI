# NalarAI: The Blue Learning Quest That Turns Curiosity Into Momentum

## 1. The Origin Story (Vision & Problem Statement)

Learning on the internet has lived through a strange dark age. Students can summon an ocean of information in seconds, yet they are often left alone to decide where to begin, what matters, and whether they truly understood anything. Traditional course platforms stack hour-long videos into intimidating libraries. Generic chatbots answer instantly, but an instant answer can become a shortcut around the very thinking that creates durable knowledge. Progress disappears between devices, motivation fades after the first burst of enthusiasm, and the learner is left with ten open tabs and no clear next step.

NalarAI was created to break that pattern. Its grand vision is to make serious understanding feel as approachable as opening a favorite mobile game. A learner names any topic, and the system transforms that curiosity into a visible path of small, deliberate victories. Each level contains a focused explanation, a memorable takeaway, and a quiz that asks the learner to retrieve and apply the idea. The result is not an endless feed. It is a guided expedition where every node has a purpose.

The blue visual identity reinforces that promise. Sapphire communicates clarity, trust, and forward motion, while tactile buttons, friendly shapes, and Gara, the crowned blue Garuda guide, remove the anxiety that often surrounds difficult subjects. NalarAI is playful without trivializing education and professional without becoming cold.

## 2. The Heroes of the Story (Target Audience)

The first heroes are curious Indonesian learners who know what they want to explore but do not know how to structure the journey. They might be students preparing for a class, professionals refreshing a concept, or lifelong learners following a question that appeared during the day. Their shared pain is not a shortage of content. It is the absence of a trusted sequence.

NalarAI also serves learners whose motivation arrives in short windows. They do not always have an uninterrupted hour, but they can protect five focused minutes. Bite-sized levels respect that reality. Hearts make each answer feel meaningful, streaks turn consistency into something visible, and weekly leagues create a gentle sense of community without turning education into a punishing competition.

For the learner who has been discouraged by a wrong answer, NalarAI changes the emotional contract. A mistake is not a dead end or a red mark. It is a clue. The system explains why the correct choice works and immediately turns confusion into a stronger mental model.

## 3. Under the Hood: The Architectural Masterpiece

The frontend is powered by Next.js App Router and React, functioning like the precision cockpit of a high-performance learning machine. The public landing page and authenticated application share one visual engine, so there is no jarring handoff from marketing promise to product reality. Tailwind CSS supplies the chassis, while a purpose-built sapphire token system controls every radius, shadow, spacing decision, and accessible state. The result feels fast because server-rendered structure arrives early and client interactivity is reserved for the places that genuinely need it.

The server is the disciplined navigator. It owns authentication, validation, lesson generation, answer checking, and reward calculation. Instead of handing an AI credential to the browser, NalarAI acts as an airlock. It composes a tightly constrained prompt, calls Google Gemini through the official server SDK, demands structured JSON, and validates the entire payload before the lesson is allowed into the product.

MongoDB is the expedition log. Users, opaque sessions, progress, gamification statistics, lesson sessions, and rate-limit buckets each have a focused collection. Mongoose is the cartographer that gives these flexible documents strict boundaries and indexes. TTL indexes behave like an automated cleanup crew, quietly removing expired sessions, temporary lessons, and rate-limit windows without manual maintenance.

The system is deliberately modular rather than prematurely fragmented into microservices. For the current product stage, a well-structured Next.js application keeps delivery fast, transactions understandable, and deployment economical. The boundaries are already clear enough that AI generation or competitive leaderboards can move into dedicated services when real scale demands it.

## 4. The User Journey (How the Magic Happens)

The journey begins on a bright, spacious landing page. The learner immediately sees the core promise, a live preview of the winding path, and an install control that can place NalarAI on a phone or desktop. One action creates an account. The registration gate checks email structure, verifies that the domain can receive mail, hashes the password, creates the learner's progress and gamification records, and returns an opaque session cookie that JavaScript cannot read.

Inside the application, the learner chooses a topic and touches the glowing active node. That small click starts a carefully protected chain reaction. The browser sends only the topic and stage. The server checks the session, rate limit, remaining hearts, and request shape. It then asks Gemini for three compact material cards and four multiple-choice questions in strict JSON. The response is inspected, transformed into internal IDs, and stored in MongoDB.

Here is the critical magic trick: the browser receives the lesson but not the answer key. The correct choices remain in the server-side vault. When the learner commits an answer, the server permits only one attempt for that question, reveals the explanation, and records the result. At the final question, the server calculates the score, deducts hearts for misses, awards XP, updates the streak, opens the next path node, and stamps the lesson session so the same victory cannot be claimed twice.

The interface responds instantly with a celebration screen. The league and profile views reflect the same source of truth. If the learner installs the PWA, the experience launches like a native application, while personalized pages remain protected from service-worker caching.

## 5. Enterprise-Grade Superpowers (Security, Scaling, & QA)

NalarAI follows a zero-trust posture. Every browser payload is treated as a suggestion until the server validates it. Passwords are protected with bcrypt. Session verifiers are random, hashed, peppered, short-lived, and revocable. Mutation routes check browser origin signals. Security headers block framing, plugin content, MIME confusion, and unnecessary device capabilities.

The AI boundary has its own armor. Requests time out, oversized responses are rejected, markdown-wrapped output is refused, and Zod guards every string, array, option count, and answer index. This prevents malformed generations from leaking unpredictability into the user experience. Answer keys are private, and the server never trusts a score calculated by the client.

Reliability comes from idempotency. If a mobile connection retries the final answer, the generated session ID acts like a claim ticket: the reward can be collected once and only once. Set-based progress updates prevent duplicate completed levels. MongoDB TTL indexes clean temporary state, and lazy heart regeneration means the system can recover hearts without a permanent scheduler.

Quality shifts left into the developer workflow. Strict TypeScript catches contract drift. ESLint enforces React and accessibility discipline. A production Next.js build proves every route can compile and render. Playwright launches an already installed local Chromium browser, then stress-tests the landing page, authentication, app panels, lesson dialogs, install prompts, touch targets, and horizontal containment from an ultra-narrow 280-pixel viewport to a 1,440-pixel desktop. The dependency audit closes the loop by checking the supply chain before deployment.

## 6. The Evolution (Changelog & Latest Iteration)

The earlier architecture iteration was a complete level-up from the Vite prototype. The former client-only micro-course and duplicate Express functions were removed. In their place now stands a unified Next.js App Router product with a real public landing route, a protected application route, server APIs, and one coherent deployment model.

The visual experience was rebuilt around the supplied mobile reference. NalarAI now has floating streak, XP, and heart pills; a winding five-node path; large quiz cards; clear selected and feedback states; a sapphire league; an activity profile; a responsive local character cast; premium desktop compositions; and mobile-safe navigation. The green prototype identity was replaced by a focused modern blue system that flows continuously from the landing page into the learning app.

Authentication advanced from nonexistent to a secure email-and-password system with domain checks, bcrypt hashing, persistent opaque sessions, revocation, and abuse limits. MongoDB now holds dedicated user, progress, gamification, session, lesson, and rate-limit records.

AI generation moved out of the browser and behind a guarded server adapter. The new flow validates raw JSON, hides answer keys, records one answer per question, and awards progress idempotently. This closes the most important integrity gap in the prototype: the client can no longer declare its own correct answers or XP.

PWA support also moved from a manifest-only label to a complete install experience. Both public and authenticated surfaces expose install controls, icons are available in standard and maskable sizes, an offline screen exists, and the service worker obeys strict privacy boundaries. Finally, local-Chromium Playwright coverage now runs successfully across desktop and mobile without downloading a browser binary.

An earlier iteration repaired a local sign-in roadblock without opening the security gate. The server had confused its own backstage address with the front door used by the learner's browser, rejecting legitimate requests as cross-site traffic. Development now checks the real request host, while production checks the explicitly configured public origin. Foreign domains, forged forwarding headers, mismatched ports, and malformed origins remain outside the gate.

The newest level-up rebuilds responsiveness as an architectural rule. The old 320-pixel floor is gone, every major grid track can truly shrink, and one shared safe-area frame keeps content clear of phone notches in portrait and landscape. The public header now folds into a focused mobile menu instead of squeezing links off-screen. The app menu floats over short viewports instead of stealing the learning area. Marketing previews, league rows, loading skeletons, and full-screen lessons all reflow without horizontal scrolling from 280 pixels through wide desktop screens.

Usability advanced at the same time. Mobile controls now expose at least a 48-by-48-pixel target. Muted, success, danger, and placeholder colors were strengthened for readable contrast. Authentication tabs now keep their heading, URL, password visibility, field errors, and keyboard controls synchronized. Quiz choices behave like a real radio group, arrow keys move between answers, and each new lesson step is announced through managed focus. The AI generation screen became a true modal boundary, private PWA launches now fall back to the offline screen, and the loading view mirrors the final application shell so content no longer jumps into place. In short, this iteration did not merely repaint NalarAI; it gave the interface a suspension system that absorbs tiny screens, long text, touch input, keyboards, and interrupted networks without losing control.

The latest refinement attacks scroll fatigue on the public landing page. The towering phone mockup was retired and replaced by a compact learning cockpit that communicates topic, progress, hearts, streak, and the next action in seconds. Mobile now speaks in headlines rather than paragraphs: shorter calls to action, two essential proof points, compact three-step rows, and feature copy written specifically for a small screen. The hero begins immediately below the top bar instead of floating in a large empty gap. Every major landing section is now guarded by a viewport-density test across 320-pixel phones, standard mobile screens, short laptops, and wide desktops, so one idea stays visible at a time without forcing the learner through oversized sections.

Brand identity received its own level-up. The crowned blue Gara illustration is now the single visual signature across navigation, browser tabs, Apple home screens, installed PWA launchers, shortcuts, offline caches, and social previews. Rather than forcing one heavy source image into every context, the build turns it into a family of purpose-sized assets: a tightly cropped UI mark for instant recognition, crisp favicon and touch variants, high-resolution launcher icons, and a mask-safe edition that survives the circles, rounded squares, and adaptive shapes imposed by different operating systems. The result is one recognizable character everywhere without wasting bandwidth or sacrificing sharpness.

The newest evolution turns that single mascot into a culturally local coaching ensemble. Gara leads the path and celebrates mastery. Putri bursts into correct-answer cards and league promotions. Rara delivers firm but constructive feedback after an early mistake, while Bima takes over after three wrong answers or inside difficult mathematics, physics, and coding modules. Mang Ujang calmly prepares AI lessons and nudges the daily target; Nenek Sri appears only when the active streak truly reaches thirty days. These are not random decorations. They are selected from real lesson, streak, reward, and promotion state, animated with motion that automatically quiets itself for reduced-motion users. A reproducible Sharp pipeline converts all sixty-eight transparent source stickers into stable square assets, preventing layout shifts while keeping every expression crisp.

The latest refactor gives that ensemble a permanent seat beside the learner. At tablet and desktop widths, the learning room now has three clear zones: navigation on the left, one focused task in the center, and real profile statistics plus a live companion on the right. On a phone, the same companion moves above the question and navigation moves under the thumb. Instead of piling fixed overlays on top of answers, the viewport reserves separate rows for scrolling content, feedback, and navigation. The result feels like a calm study desk rather than a crowded control panel.

Four typed building blocks now drive the experience: the learning layout arranges the room, the question card presents accessible single-choice answers, the companion gives every new card a spring entrance and every emotion a gentle crossfade, and the feedback bar slides into its own action row. Correct answers release a small burst of confetti around Putri, never across the whole screen. Mistakes bring Rara's reality check or Bima's reassurance with a restrained shake. Motion preferences are synchronized safely after server hydration, so learners who prefer reduced motion get stable, immediate reactions without bounce or confetti.

Help became a real interaction, not an answer shortcut. Mang Ujang or Nenek Sri rises in a keyboard-contained hint sheet and revisits the key takeaways already taught in the lesson. No hidden answer key is fetched and no answer is submitted by asking for help. Feedback respects the same honesty: XP appears only when the server sends an actual completion award, including a zero award, while intermediate answers explain that XP is calculated at the end. Failed requests preserve the selected answer without pretending the learner made a mistake. Focus moves to Continue after feedback mounts and returns to the original hint button after the sheet closes, keeping both touch and keyboard journeys coherent.

The latest reliability repair fixes a small packaging mismatch that stopped an otherwise healthy AI response at the door. The provider sometimes wrapped lesson JSON in a Markdown code fence, while NalarAI accepted only unwrapped JSON. The adapter now unwraps one complete JSON envelope and then applies exactly the same strict lesson schema. Extra prose, executable language blocks, multiple envelopes, broken JSON, unexpected fields, and invalid answer indices still fail validation. Think of it as accepting a sealed letter inside an envelope without relaxing the checks on what the letter actually says. Provider transport failures and malformed lessons now have distinct error codes and sanitized server diagnostics instead of an ambiguous gateway error.

Database updates also moved to the current Mongoose return-document API without changing reward or progress behavior. The browser security investigation kept the same discipline: direct application responses allow Next.js scripts, while frame and object protection stay enabled. The reported script-denial policy was absent from the app configuration and direct response headers, with simulator-frame messages pointing toward an external preview context. New regression tests check both the real document policy and successful browser hydration, so a simulator warning is not mistaken for permission to dismantle production security.

The newest provider migration moves lesson generation to Google Gemini after the previous proxy reported that all of its nodes had failed. NalarAI uses the official server SDK with the requested `gemini-3.5-flash-lite` model, a separate system instruction, temperature 0.7, and minimal thinking. Gemini is asked for structured JSON matching the lesson schema, and Zod still performs an independent second inspection before anything reaches MongoDB. The API key stays behind the server boundary, provider diagnostics are sanitized, retries are bounded, and an opt-in live smoke test uses the exact production integration. This replaces a fragile proxy hop with a direct, testable contract while preserving private answer keys.

## 7. The Road Ahead (Future Roadmap)

The next sprint should complete email ownership with OTP verification, password reset, session-device management, and account recovery. That turns strong domain assurance into verified mailbox control.

The learning engine can then evolve beyond a fixed five-node arc. A diagnostic opening quiz could estimate prior knowledge and allow the AI to branch the path: accelerate through familiar concepts, slow down around misconceptions, and schedule spaced-review nodes at the moment memory begins to fade.

For schools and families, the natural expansion is a mentor dashboard that highlights effort, consistency, and misconception patterns without exposing private learning conversations. For platform scale, leaderboards can move to time-bucketed ranking infrastructure, AI generation can gain a queue and content-quality review layer, and observability can track latency, schema failures, and learning outcomes.

The destination is larger than another study app. NalarAI can become a personal map for lifelong curiosity: always installed, always ready, and always able to turn one question into the next meaningful victory.
